A spyglass over four centuries

Utrecht through time

Jacob van Deventer surveyed the cities of the Low Countries for Philip II of Spain. His 1569/70 plan of Utrecht is among the earliest detailed ground plans of the city. This lens places that plan — and the topographical surveys that followed — over the present-day city, so streets, canals and fortifications can be compared directly.

How it works

  • Move your pointer and a spyglass reveals a historic plan — 1569/70, 1900, 1925, 1950 or 1975 — exactly where you point.
  • Stop moving and the lens slowly grows to fill the screen; move again and it shrinks back.
  • Swap flips the layers: the old plan stays on screen and today's city appears inside the lens.
  • The lens is 1:1 with the base map — nothing is magnified — so the streets line up exactly.
